About SWS

Trusted Well Logging Experience Built Over Decades

Statewide Well Surveys LLC provides professional geophysical well logging services for water wells, monitoring wells, engineering firms, hydrogeologists, municipalities, and drilling contractors throughout the Western United States.

Based in Yucaipa, California, with over 30 years of industry experience, SWS delivers dependable field operations, modern calibrated equipment, and professional reporting designed to support quality groundwater and engineering projects.

All logs are collected and processed on the industry-standard SDS Warrior platform, ensuring consistent, professional deliverables on every project.

Technology & Standards

Equipment, Safety & Quality Assurance

Equipment & Acquisition Platforms

Statewide Well Surveys LLC operates a dual-platform digital acquisition system, running both SDS Warrior and Robertson Geologging units. This gives us the flexibility to match the right tool string to each project's hole size, depth, and formation conditions — and to keep a fully redundant backup system on every job, so a single piece of equipment is never a reason to delay or reschedule.

All of our sondes are modern 12-channel digital tools, factory-calibrated and field-checked before each job. Digital acquisition captures a continuous, high-resolution data stream in real time, producing cleaner curves and immediate on-site quality review — advantages older analog strip-chart systems can't match. Every client receives digital LAS deliverables that are easy to archive, reprocess, and share with engineers, regulators, or other contractors.

Safety, Compliance & Quality Assurance

Our field crews are trained to OSHA and MSHA safety standards and complete site-specific safety orientations, including Job Safety Analyses (JSAs), before work begins on each location. Thorough field documentation — from safety checklists to daily job records — gives clients and regulatory agencies a clear paper trail for every well logged.

Quality assurance is built into our routine: every tool undergoes scheduled inspection and calibration to manufacturer specifications, with calibration records kept on file for each job. This systematic approach to equipment care and safety compliance keeps projects on schedule and ensures the data we deliver is accurate, repeatable, and ready for technical or regulatory review.
