Acquisition Platforms & Tools

Advanced Digital Logging Technology

Statewide Well Surveys utilizes both the industry-proven SDS Warrior platform and the Robertson Geologging Micrologger. This dual-platform approach provides maximum flexibility, redundancy, and data quality for our clients.

Platform 01 — SDS Warrior

Industry-Standard Flexibility

The SDS Warrior is the industry standard across groundwater and geophysical logging. It runs tools from multiple manufacturers, so we're never locked into one equipment provider.

That flexibility matters across the varied terrain and well conditions we encounter from California to New Mexico.

Platform 02 — Robertson Geologging (Newest)

Fully Digital 12-Channel Technology

Our Robertson Geologging tools are fully digital. Calibration coefficients stay native to the tool — no intermediate conversions, no introduced errors. What goes down the hole comes back as clean, factory-certified data.

  • Fully digital 12-channel acquisition
  • Certified factory calibrations with laboratory-based procedures
  • Direct digital tool-to-surface communication
  • Reduced signal degradation and electrical noise
  • Improved repeatability and accuracy
Technical Details — Click to Expand

Older Analog Systems

Many older geophysical logging systems rely on analog measurements that must be converted multiple times before final processing. Each conversion is an opportunity for calibration drift, signal loss, or data error — reducing confidence in the final dataset.

Robertson Digital Platform

Statewide's Robertson platform transmits measurements directly from the downhole tool to the acquisition system in digital form. Calibration coefficients remain native to the tool throughout the logging process, preserving factory-certified accuracy and reducing data error.

The result is greater confidence in the final dataset and improved consistency between surveys.

  • Access to a wide range of industry-proven logging tools from multiple manufacturers
  • State-of-the-art digital acquisition with factory-certified calibrations
  • Enhanced operational flexibility — tailor logging solutions to each project
  • Equipment redundancy: multiple platforms minimize field downtime
  • Improved data integrity and quality assurance across all survey types
  • Operational continuity as technology continues to evolve

Statewide Well Surveys maintains multiple acquisition platforms and backup logging systems to minimize downtime and maximize project reliability. Our combination of SDS Warrior and Robertson Geologging systems allows us to select the most appropriate technology for each application while maintaining operational continuity in the field.

Logging equipment is routinely inspected, maintained, and calibrated in accordance with manufacturer recommendations and industry best practices. Factory-certified calibrations are maintained where applicable to help ensure accurate and reliable results.
